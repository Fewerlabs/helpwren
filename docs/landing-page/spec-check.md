# Landing page: check against the brief

Every requirement in `docs/helpwren-landing-page-prompt.md`, the owner's later requests, and the
two independent reviews' findings, one row each. A row says **Done** only with evidence beside it.
Checked on the production build in Chromium:

- **Overlap check** (`apps/site/scripts/overlap-check.mjs`): the page scrolled in 20px steps; at
  every step the wren's box is compared with every heading, paragraph, caption, list item and
  code block on screen, and with the screen's edges while it flies. Output for 640, 800, 900,
  1024, 1280, 1366 and 1440 wide, light and dark: [`overlap-check.txt`](overlap-check.txt).
- **Stress test** (kept with the review evidence): a 120px wheel scan of the whole page, watching
  for jumps, words under the wren and the wren off screen; reloads at five depths; resizes
  mid-scroll; an anchor jump past the nest and back; fast flicks; at 800, 1024, 1280 and 1440.

Screenshots are in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/):

- `flight-<width>-<theme>`: the wren mid-flight on every leg, three stills per leg (at 20%, 50%
  and 80% of its flight), at 800, 1024, 1280 and 1440, light and dark.
- `wren-frames-vs-logo`: every drawn frame beside the logo.
- `header-footer-1280`, `sticky-header-1280`, `phone-perch-arrives-*`, `no-js-*`,
  `reduced-motion-1280`.

Videos of a full scroll with the wheel are in [`video/`](video/): `wren-800-light.webm` and
`wren-1280-light.webm`.

**Done, changed** means the brief's words could not be followed literally without saying
something untrue about the product, or the owner asked for something else; the row says which.
