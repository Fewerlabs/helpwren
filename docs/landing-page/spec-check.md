# Landing page: check against the brief

Every requirement in `docs/helpwren-landing-page-prompt.md`, the owner's later requests, and the
independent review's findings, one row each. Checked in a browser on the production build, and by
the stress test (`stress.mjs`, kept with the review evidence): a 40px wheel scan of the whole page
watching for jumps, reloads at five depths, resizes mid-scroll, an anchor jump past the nest and
back, and fast flicks, at 375, 768, 1280 and 1440, light and dark.

Screenshots are in [`screenshots/`](screenshots/):

- `seq-<width>-<theme>-NN`: the page scrolled with the wheel, top (00) to bottom.
- `perched-light-<perch>`: just after the wren lands at each perch.
- `wren-3d-<pose>`: close-ups of the 3D wren.
- `no-js-*`, `reduced-motion-*`: full pages.

Videos of a full scroll are in [`video/`](video/): `desktop-1280-light.webm` and
`phone-375-light.webm`.

**Done, changed** means the brief's words could not be followed literally without saying
something untrue about the product, or the owner asked for something else; the row says which.
