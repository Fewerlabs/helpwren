Status, for the inbox

Claude Design had two. A help desk needs five.
Open Biro blue #2F5B85 on #E0E8F1 Open, links, internal notes
Waiting Ochre #7D5A12 on #F4EBD6 Waiting on the user
Resolved Moss #3D6B57 on #E4EDE7 Resolved, online, verified
Urgent Red pen #B23520 on #F7E1DB High priority, errors
Closed Pencil #6E675B on #EDE9E0 Closed, archived, offline

Every chip pairs colour with a word and a dot, so status never depends on colour alone. All pass 4.5:1.
