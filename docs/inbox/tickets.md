# Tickets

Track requests that take more than one reply with a number, status, priority and assignee.

A ticket is a conversation with more structure. It keeps the same thread, so the contact keeps
writing in the chat as before.

## Create a ticket

- **From the inbox**: open a conversation and choose **Convert to ticket**, then give it a
  subject.
- **From the chat**: turn on **Let people submit a ticket from the chat** in **Settings →
  General**. The chat's home screen then offers **Submit a ticket** beside **Send us a message**,
  with a form for a subject and details.

Each ticket gets the next number, such as `#1042`.

## Fields

| Field    | Values                          |
| -------- | ------------------------------- |
| Status   | Open, Waiting, Resolved, Closed |
| Priority | Low, Normal (the default), High |
| Assignee | A team member, or Unassigned    |
| Team     | One of your teams, or No team   |
| Subject  | One line, up to 200 characters  |

The **Tickets** view lists every ticket as a table.

## Keep the contact informed

With email set up, the contact is emailed when a ticket's status changes, if **Changes to a
ticket's status** is on in **Settings → Email**. They can reply to that email, and the reply lands
in the ticket. See [Email](../email/index.md) and [Replies by email](../email/replies.md).
