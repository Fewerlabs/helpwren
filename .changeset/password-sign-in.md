Team members can sign in with a password as well as an emailed code. `auth.signInWithPassword`
and `auth.setPassword` in `@helpwren/core`, with `passwordSchema`; a `PasswordInput` with a
show-password button, and a `password` field type for `FieldList`, in the design system.
