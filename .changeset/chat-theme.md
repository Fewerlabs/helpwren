The chat can be dark. `Helpwren.init({ theme })` and `SupportScreen`'s `theme` take `light` (the default, as before), `dark`, or `system` to follow the visitor's device, and `Helpwren.update({ theme })` or a new prop switches the open chat without reloading it. The widget's panel matches.
