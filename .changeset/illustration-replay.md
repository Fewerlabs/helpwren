`Wren` and `Sketch` take `replay`: each time it goes up, the drawing draws again and plays its
small action. Scenes gained small actions of their own: the bell rings, the telescope sweeps, the
bookmark drops into the book, and `hw-pop` pops a badge in a sketch. The plug in the "plugs-in"
scene is its own group (`hw-plug`), so a page can move it into the socket.
